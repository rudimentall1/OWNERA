# OWNERA Product Tiers

Pricing is intentionally separated from security authority.

A paid plan may unlock product capabilities, integrations, automation quotas, or advanced analytics. **No plan can override constitutional safety rules or grant access to the Sovereign Reserve.**

| Tier | Positioning | Autonomous scope |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Observer | Analyze, monitor, recommend |
| Autopilot | Routine finance | Recurring bills/subscriptions within hard limits |
| Personal | Personal financial agent | Voice transfers and broader payment workflows |
| Autonomous | Autonomous treasury | Policy-bounded investment/DeFi execution |
| Sovereign | Maximum autonomy | Advanced security, recovery, crypto-agility and treasury automation |
| Enterprise | Institutional/family treasury | Multiple identities, policies, audit, API and organization controls |

## Pricing principles

1. The user pays for **autonomy and service**, not for permission to bypass safety.
2. Security invariants are identical across paid tiers.
3. The Sovereign Reserve is never a premium feature.
4. A subscription can be revoked without changing ownership of assets or the user's constitutional rules.
5. Product entitlements are evaluated independently from authorization policy.
6. Regulated financial execution should be routed through appropriately authorized providers until OWNERA itself has the required permissions.

## Recommended commercial model

Start with a generous Free tier and convert users through progressively higher autonomy:

```text
FREE
  ↓
AUTOPILOT
  ↓
PERSONAL
  ↓
AUTONOMOUS
  ↓
SOVEREIGN
```

Potential future monetization:

- monthly subscription;
- additional integration bundles;
- advanced security package;
- enterprise/API plans;
- transparent execution fees where appropriate;
- performance-linked pricing only where legally permissible and clearly disclosed.

The product should never make financial safety dependent on payment status. Safety gates remain active for every user.
