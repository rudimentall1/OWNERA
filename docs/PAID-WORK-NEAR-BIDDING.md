# NEAR Agent Market bounded bidding

\`ownera paid-work bid-near-market\` evaluates the live public standard-job board.
Without \`--submit\` it is read-only and prints eligible candidates. A live bid
requires both \`--submit\` and \`OWNERA_NEAR_BIDDING_ENABLED=true\`.

## Initial policy defaults

- USDC only. USD bids are skipped because they require a configured Stripe
  payout account.
- Bid amount must be between 5 and 25 USDC and uses the posted job budget.
- Standard public jobs only, with an open slot.
- Builder verification must be \`none\`; verifiable delivery must not be required.
- Listing age is limited to 30 days.
- Capability overlap is required for Python, JavaScript, API integration,
  automation, data processing, research, or security review.
- Excludes work that appears to require bookings, purchases, calls, transfers,
  trading, regulated advice, or ongoing hosting.
- At most two pending bids and one active assignment. By default, pending bids
  plus active assignments share a single total commitment slot, preventing two
  simultaneous awards from overloading the worker. One new bid per run.
- No explicit SLA is sent; the marketplace applies the agent's configured
  default SLA.
- Proposal text is generated by the local Ollama model. It must not claim
  credentials, prior customers, paid services, or capabilities not established
  by the brief.

Override numeric limits through the \`OWNERA_NEAR_BID_*\` environment variables.
The policy is deliberately fail-closed and is not a general promise that every
job is safe or profitable.

## Commands

\`\`\`bash
ownera paid-work bid-near-market
OWNERA_NEAR_BIDDING_ENABLED=true ownera paid-work bid-near-market --submit
\`\`\`

The first command is read-only. The second sends real bids. The systemd timer
must not be enabled until assignment execution and recovery are also deployed.

## Important boundary

A bid is an offer, not a completed order. The buyer's acceptance creates the
job agreement. Current assignment intake and marketplace delivery exist, but
automatic assignment planning/execution is not yet a complete unattended
workflow. Do not enable recurring bidding until the executor can reliably
start, execute, independently review, submit, and reconcile a real assignment.

## Current bid terms check

The submitted 15 USDC bid is still pending. The live job board reports an
acceptance window of 3 days, while the job description says 24 hours. The
current Standardized Job Terms state 3–7 days and make the job-posting field
controlling for a bid award. This discrepancy should be reported in the
research deliverable rather than silently treating 24 hours as the platform
rule. The terms also say a Dispute Deposit is staked only by the party that
initiates a dispute; it is not an automatic upfront fee. The current terms
state 5% of the job budget and do not state the 0.01 minimum mentioned in the
brief.

## Execution dependency

The current Gensyn2 Ollama endpoint did not complete a 90-token proposal
generation within 60 seconds using qwen2.5:0.5b, including an 8-thread attempt.
The bid path therefore uses deterministic, scope-limited proposal templates.
The existing Docker worker is network-isolated and requires a trusted
AssignmentExecutionSpec; no general task planner or dependable inference
provider is currently configured. Recurring bidding must remain disabled until
a real assignment can be planned, executed, independently reviewed, delivered,
and reconciled without manual intervention.
